The air changes
before the sky does.

“Smoke arrives before I understand what is missing.”

FIELD NOTE 03

Koala populations in Queensland, New South Wales and the ACT are nationally listed as endangered. Habitat loss, climate pressures and bushfire impacts are among the threats identified in government guidance.
